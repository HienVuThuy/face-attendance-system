## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM Assessment:** The interface has a strong, authentic identity tailored specifically to IoT biometric attendance. The distinction between the dark command rail (#0f172a) and high-density telemetry cards (#ffffff on #f1f5f9) feels purposeful rather than generic template slop. The addition of the 128-d Face Descriptor preview and hardware countdown indicators gives the dashboard immediate domain authority.

**Deterministic Scan (detect.mjs):** 15 findings detected across `dashboard/src`:
- 12 instances of `gray-on-color` (`text-slate-600/700` over tinted badges and buttons in Header, AttendanceLogs, and StudentManagement).
- 2 instances of `ai-color-palette` (`from-purple-600` gradient in LiveCamera).
- 1 instance of `overused-font` (`Inter`).

## Overall Impression

A production-grade, highly polished IoT admin dashboard that excels at real-time telemetry and biometric feedback. The biggest opportunities lie in fixing micro-contrast on tinted badge buttons, adding deletion confirmation guardrails, and replacing generic purple gradients with brand blue/indigo tokens.

## What's Working
- **Real-Time Biometric Feedback:** Seamless visual distinction between verified student check-ins (Emerald) and unrecognized strangers (Rose) with live countdowns.
- **Bi-directional Navigation & Profile Controls:** Full administrative profile popover, instant tab redirection, and real-time notification feed.
- **Hardware Simulation Fallback:** Graceful degradation when ESP32 hardware is disconnected, preventing demo lockouts.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Low Contrast on Tinted Badges (`gray-on-color`)**
  - *Why it matters:* Using `text-slate-600/700` on colored backgrounds (`bg-blue-100`, `bg-emerald-500`, `bg-amber-500`) reduces readability on low-contrast projectors and bright monitors.
  - *Fix:* Replace with tone-on-tone dark text (`text-blue-800 on bg-blue-100`, `text-emerald-900 on bg-emerald-50`, `text-white on bg-emerald-500`).
  - *Suggested command:* `/impeccable polish dashboard`

- **[P2] Student Deletion Safety Guardrail**
  - *Why it matters:* Deleting a registered student removes their 128-d vector immediately, risking accidental data loss during live demonstrations.
  - *Fix:* Implement a two-step confirmation modal before student deletion.
  - *Suggested command:* `/impeccable harden dashboard`

- **[P2] Fast Keyboard Navigation & Accelerators**
  - *Why it matters:* Power users (Alex) need fast keyboard access (`/` to focus search, `Esc` to close all modals, `N` for new student).
  - *Fix:* Add global hotkey listeners for search and modal management.
  - *Suggested command:* `/impeccable delight dashboard`

- **[P3] Replace Generic Purple AI Gradients**
  - *Why it matters:* Purple/violet gradients trigger automated AI slop detection and diverge from the Electric Blue/Midnight Slate design system in DESIGN.md.
  - *Fix:* Switch to `from-blue-600 to-indigo-600` gradients in LiveCamera.
  - *Suggested command:* `/impeccable quieter dashboard`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Alex (Power User / Admin):** No keyboard shortcut to quickly jump to the search box or open the "+ Thêm Sinh viên" modal without reaching for the mouse.
- **Jordan (First-Timer / Teacher):** Needs an inline tooltip explaining what the "128-d Vector AI" badge means when enrolling a student's photo.
- **Riley (Methodical Stress Tester):** Deleting a student happens in 1 click with no confirmation dialog or undo option.

## Minor Observations
- Active tab indicator on Sidebar has smooth transition, but could have a subtle glowing border for extra precision.
- Notification bell popover could include a "Đánh dấu đã đọc" (Mark all as read) button.
